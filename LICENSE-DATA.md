## Not covered

- **Images.** The data files list image URLs and credits, not images. Each photo keeps
  its own license, recorded in `image_license` and `avicommons_image.license`. Many are
  non-commercial, and Macaulay Library photos are © Macaulay Library. Check the license
  of each image before you reuse it.

## Credit

Please credit the version you used, for example:
*BirdNET+ Taxonomy v0.4-Oct2026, https://birdnet.cornell.edu/taxonomy, CC BY-SA 4.0*.

The data draws on these sources:

- **Descriptions:** [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org) contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  through the articles in `wikipedia_urls`, including LLM translations and summaries of
  those articles. Sound class descriptions come from the
  [AudioSet ontology](https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/) by Google
  (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Bird taxonomy:** AviList Core Team, 2026. AviList: The Global Avian Checklist, v2026.
  [doi:10.2173/avilist.v2026](https://doi.org/10.2173/avilist.v2026) (CC BY 4.0).
- **Names and identifiers:** [eBird and the eBird/Clements Checklist](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/clementschecklist/),
  [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org), [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0),
  [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org), [Catalogue of Life](https://www.catalogueoflife.org),
  [Macaulay Library](https://www.macaulaylibrary.org),
  [Xeno-Canto](https://xeno-canto.org), and [observation.org](https://observation.org).
